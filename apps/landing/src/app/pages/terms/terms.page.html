3. Listings and pricing

Designers are responsible for the accuracy of their listings, including photographs of their own work, base prices, fabric and add-on pricing, size-based adjustments and estimated production times. The total shown to the customer at checkout is binding for that order.

4. Orders and payment

Placing an order creates an offer to the designer, who may accept or reject it. Payment is taken at checkout through our payment provider and held by Steeze in escrow. Funds are not released to the designer at the point of payment. If a designer rejects an order, the customer is refunded in full.

5. Delivery

When a designer marks an order ready, Steeze dispatches a courier to collect it from the designer's shop address and deliver it to the address on the order. Delivery timescales are estimates and depend on the courier and the destination.

6. Confirmation and release of funds

The customer has 2 calendar days from delivery to confirm the order or request a return. Confirming releases the funds to the designer, less the platform commission. If the customer takes no action within that window, the order auto-confirms and funds are released on the same basis.

7. Returns

A return may be requested within the 2-day window with a stated reason. Steeze reviews the request and, where accepted, dispatches a courier to collect the garment from the customer and return it to the designer. Because garments are made to order against the customer's measurements, change-of-mind returns are not guaranteed. Where a return is accepted on the basis that the work was not up to standard, the flat return courier fee is deducted from the designer's held funds.

8. Fees

Steeze charges designers a commission on completed orders and a flat return courier fee in the circumstances described above. Current rates are published on our pricing page and shown in the designer dashboard. Rate changes apply to orders placed after the change takes effect.

9. Ratings

Ratings unlock for both parties once an order is confirmed, so they can only be left by people who completed a transaction together. Ratings must be honest and must not contain abusive content. Steeze may remove ratings that breach this.
