Sell your designs on Steeze

You make the clothes. Steeze finds the customers, collects the money, arranges the courier and handles the awkward conversations when an order goes wrong.

What you get

A catalogue that sells for you

Upload photos, set a base price, and define the fabrics, add-ons and size-based pricing you offer. Customers see the true total before they order.

Orders you can plan around

Accept or reject each order, set your own turnaround, and move it through in-progress and ready-for-pickup as you work.

Payment you can count on

The customer pays before you cut a single piece of fabric. The money is already held by Steeze when the order reaches you.

Couriers arranged for you

Mark an order ready and Steeze dispatches a courier to your shop. You never have to negotiate delivery.

A rating that reflects real work

Only customers who completed an order with you can rate you, and you rate them back. No drive-by reviews.

Earnings in one place

Track held funds, released payouts and deductions from your dashboard instead of reconciling transfers by hand.

Getting started

  1. 1

    Register as a designer

    Sign up with your business name and shop address. Your shop address is where couriers collect from.

  2. 2

    Get verified

    The Steeze team reviews new designer profiles. Verified designers carry a badge on their listings.

  3. 3

    Publish your first designs

    Add photos, fabrics, add-ons and sizes. Keep a design unpublished while you are still setting it up.

  4. 4

    Start taking orders

    You are notified as orders come in, and paid once each customer confirms delivery.

What is expected of you

Accurate listings

Photos should be of your own work, and prices should reflect what you will actually charge.

Honest turnarounds

Set an estimate you can hit. Repeatedly missing it affects your standing on the platform.

Standing behind your work

If a piece is returned because the work was not up to standard, the flat return courier fee comes out of your held funds.
