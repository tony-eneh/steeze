About Steeze

Bespoke clothing has always run on trust: you pay a tailor up front and hope. Steeze replaces that with a marketplace where neither side has to gamble on the other.

The problem we set out to fix

Ordering something made to measure usually means finding a tailor by word of mouth, paying a deposit with no protection, and chasing updates by phone. Good designers lose work because new customers cannot tell them apart from bad ones, and customers lose money when an order goes wrong.

Steeze puts a marketplace in the middle. Designers get discovered on the strength of real completed work. Customers get pricing up front, status they can follow, and a payment that only moves when they say so.

How we make money

A commission on completed orders. We do not charge listing fees or subscriptions, and we do not take a cut of orders that fall through. If a designer is not earning, neither are we.

That also decides what we build: anything that helps an order get made, delivered and confirmed is worth our time.

What we hold ourselves to

Money moves last

Funds sit in escrow until the customer confirms. That constraint shapes the whole order lifecycle.

Ratings are earned

Only a completed transaction unlocks a rating, in both directions. Reputation on Steeze reflects work actually done.

Prices are whole

Fabric, add-ons, size adjustments and delivery are all in the total you see before you pay.
