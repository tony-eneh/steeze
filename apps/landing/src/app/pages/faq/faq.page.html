Frequently asked questions

The things people ask us most about ordering, paying, returning and selling on Steeze.

Ordering and payment

When is the designer actually paid?

Not when you pay. Steeze holds your payment in escrow and releases it to the designer only after you confirm the delivered order, or after the 2-day confirmation window closes.

What happens if a designer rejects my order?

The order is cancelled and your payment is refunded in full. Nothing is deducted.

Which payment methods can I use?

Checkout runs on Paystack, so any card or bank method Paystack supports in your country works.

How long will my order take?

Each design shows the designer's estimated production time in days. Courier delivery is on top of that and depends on your address.

Measurements and fit

How do I give my measurements?

Link your Open Tailor profile to your Steeze account once. Your measurements are then attached to every order you place, and you can update them at any time.

What if the fit is wrong?

Raise a return within 2 days of delivery. If the piece does not match the measurements you supplied, the return courier fee is deducted from the designer rather than charged to you.

Returns

How long do I have to return something?

2 calendar days from delivery. After that the order auto-confirms and funds are released.

Do I have to arrange the return myself?

No. Steeze dispatches a courier to collect the piece from you and return it to the designer.

Can I return something just because I changed my mind?

These are made-to-order pieces cut to your measurements, so change-of-mind returns are not guaranteed. Returns exist for work that does not match what was ordered.

For designers

What does it cost to list?

Nothing. Steeze takes a 10% commission on completed orders and charges no listing or subscription fee.

When do I get paid?

When the customer confirms the delivered order, or automatically once the 2-day window closes without a return request.

Who pays for a return courier?

Where the return is because the work was not up to standard, the flat fee is deducted from your held funds.

Can I rate customers?

Yes. Ratings are bidirectional and unlock for both parties once an order is confirmed.
