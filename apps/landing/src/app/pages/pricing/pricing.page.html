Pricing and fees

No listing fees, no monthly subscription. Steeze earns a commission on completed orders, which means we only get paid when you do.

For customers

Free

You pay the order total shown at checkout: base price, fabric, add-ons, size adjustment and delivery. Nothing is added afterwards.

For designers

10% commission

Deducted from each completed order when funds are released. Listing designs, receiving orders and using the dashboard cost nothing.

Return courier

₦2,500 flat

Charged only when a piece is returned because the work was not up to standard, and deducted from the designer's held funds.

Commission and return courier rates are set by Steeze and can change. Current rates always apply to orders placed after any change, and are shown in your dashboard.

A worked example

Base price of the design ₦45,000
Fabric upgrade ₦5,000
Delivery ₦2,500
Customer pays ₦52,500
Steeze commission (10% of the garment total) −₦5,000
Designer receives on confirmation ₦45,000

Illustrative figures. Delivery is passed through to the courier and commission is calculated on the garment total.
