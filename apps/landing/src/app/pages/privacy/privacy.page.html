Privacy policy

What Steeze collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Last updated: 12 August 2026

1. Data we collect

Account data: name, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a hash), and your role on the platform. Designer accounts also include a business name, shop address and portfolio content.

Order data: the designs you order, fabric and add-on choices, delivery addresses, order status history and the measurements linked to your order.

Payment data: card details are entered with our payment provider and are never stored on Steeze servers. We keep the transaction reference, amount, currency and status.

Device data: if you enable push notifications, the device token needed to deliver them.

Technical data: IP address and request metadata from server logs, used to keep the service secure and available.

2. Why we use it

To create and secure your account, to process orders and payments, to arrange courier collection and delivery, to notify you about order and payment events, to administer returns and disputes, to calculate ratings and earnings, and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

Only the parties needed to fulfil an order. The designer you order from receives your first name, delivery details and the measurements needed to make the garment. Couriers receive the collection and delivery addresses and contact details. Our payment provider receives what it needs to take payment. Our email and push providers receive what is needed to deliver those messages. We also share data where the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is open. Order, payment and transaction records are kept after that where we need them for accounting, tax and dispute purposes. Password reset and email verification tokens are short-lived and single-use. Device tokens are removed when you sign out or when they stop being valid.

5. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to specific processing. Some data must be retained for legal and accounting reasons even after account closure. Write to privacy@steeze.com and we will respond within one month.

6. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed, and password reset and verification tokens are stored only as hashes so a database copy does not yield usable links. Access to production data is limited to staff who need it.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your device registration. We do not use advertising cookies.

8. Children

Steeze is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and will notify you of material changes in the app or by email.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and data requests: privacy@steeze.com.
