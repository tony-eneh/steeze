How Steeze works

Steeze sits between customers and designers so neither side has to take the other on trust. Here is the full journey, including what happens when something is not right.

For customers

  1. 1

    Find a design

    Browse by category, gender, price range or designer. Every listing shows the base price, the fabrics available, optional add-ons and the designer's typical turnaround in days.

  2. 2

    Customise and add measurements

    Pick your fabric, colour and any add-ons such as embroidery or custom buttons. Link your Open Tailor measurements once and reuse them on every future order.

  3. 3

    Pay into escrow

    Checkout is handled by Paystack. The money leaves your account but is held by Steeze, not passed to the designer.

  4. 4

    The designer makes it

    You are notified when the designer accepts the order, starts work and marks it ready. If a designer rejects an order, you are refunded in full.

  5. 5

    Courier delivery

    Steeze dispatches a courier to collect from the designer's shop and deliver to your address, with status updates along the way.

  6. 6

    Confirm or return

    You have 2 calendar days from delivery to confirm you are happy or request a return. Confirming releases the funds. If you do nothing, the order auto-confirms at the end of the window.

  7. 7

    Rate each other

    Once an order is confirmed, you and the designer can each leave a rating. Ratings only come from real completed orders.

If something is wrong

Request a return

Raise a return within 2 days of delivery with a reason. Steeze reviews it and dispatches a courier to collect the piece from you.

The piece goes back

The courier returns the garment to the designer, and the order is marked returned once it arrives.

Who pays the courier

Where the work was not up to standard, the flat return courier fee is deducted from the designer's held funds rather than charged to you.
